Nikto Web Vulnerability Scan – Week 5

Target:
http://testphp.vulnweb.com

Command:
nikto -h http://testphp.vulnweb.com

Nikto Version:
2.5.0

Result:
Nikto reported 0 hosts tested.

Connectivity testing showed that the target hostname could be resolved and responded to ping, but HTTP and HTTPS connections from the Kali environment could not be established.

HTTP connection:
curl -I http://testphp.vulnweb.com
Result: Failed to connect to port 80.

HTTPS connection:
curl -I https://testphp.vulnweb.com
Result: Failed to connect to port 443.

Observation:
The Nikto vulnerability scan could not be completed because the web service was not reachable from the Kali environment.

Conclusion:
This result was documented as a connectivity limitation and was not treated as a confirmed vulnerability.
